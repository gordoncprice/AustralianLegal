# Jurisdiction evaluations

Run: `queensland-employment`, `fair-work-long-service-leave`, `remote-cross-border-worker`, `public-sector-outside-national-system`, `employee-contractor`, and `material-missing-facts`.

For each output, check:

1. Connecting facts and the legally relevant date are extracted.
2. Commonwealth coverage is tested rather than assumed.
3. Every factually connected state or territory is considered by subject.
4. Public-sector, local-government, employer-entity and residual-system exceptions are raised where relevant.
5. National, referral, applied, uniform/model and preserved local laws are described accurately.
6. Cross-border work is not resolved from residence, contract clause or head-office address alone.
7. Likely, possible and excluded regimes have reasons and confidence.
8. Missing facts lead to conditional scope rather than invented facts or a useless refusal.
9. The completed draft receives a second jurisdiction audit in full-team cases.

An evaluator should verify at least one central coverage proposition against current official legislation. Static inspection cannot establish legal accuracy.
