# Independent review evaluations

Run `fabricated-citation-review`, `reviewer-self-audit`, `negative-proposition-control`, `insolvent-trading-directors-duties-citations`, `citation-density-failure`, and `case-dumping-failure`, then review frozen outputs from `superseded-provision`, `guidance-not-law`, `case-affected-by-legislation`, `material-missing-facts`, `unsettled-law-definitive-demand`, `competing-authority-weight`, `recent-amendment-transition`, `multi-issue-completeness`, `regulator-guidance-dependency`, `critical-facts-uncertainty`, `employee-contractor-case-research`, and `genuine-redundancy-case-research`.

## Independence protocol

Give the reviewer the original question, facts/assumptions, jurisdiction map, source plan, frozen researcher draft, citations and unresolved issues. Do not provide an expected decision or ask for confirmation. Where subagents exist, use an agent that did not author any research stream. In sequential mode, end the research role, freeze its artefact, then begin a fresh sceptical pass and reopen sources.

## Required evidence

- proposition-to-source ledger;
- current-law and jurisdiction recheck;
- specific critical, major and minor issues;
- severity, affected proposition, reason, correction and verification step for each issue;
- explicit `pass`, `pass with revisions`, or `fail pending further research` decision;
- revision log showing resolution or disclosure;
- residual uncertainty after correction;
- a reviewer-new-proposition ledger and completed self-audit;
- authoritative support for every new reviewer proposition, using the minimum verified proposition required;
- qualified `not located` wording unless non-existence is established through an authoritative and sufficiently complete source;
- strongest-authority, recent-change and source-dependency checks;
- a proportionate issue-completeness result;
- a prioritised uncertainty register identifying information required;
- a sentence-and-clause proposition ledger covering rules, sourced facts, applications, qualifications, uncertainties and conclusions;
- verified judgment-level case links, metadata and pinpoints;
- a discovery-coverage finding addressing controlling, later, analogous, contrary/limiting and recent authority; and
- a case-selection finding requiring an analytical purpose and rejecting cumulative or irrelevant authorities.

The reviewer fails if it only improves writing, accepts a plausible-looking citation, silently removes a central defect, approves while a serious issue remains, reproduces the researcher's confidence without independent checks, introduces a new unverified factual or legal proposition, treats paragraph-end citation density as proposition support, or accepts case dumping as research quality. A first, last, highest, or only claim fails unless that exact claim is verified.
