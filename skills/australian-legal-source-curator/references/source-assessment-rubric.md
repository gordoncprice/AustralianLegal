# Source assessment rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2 and explain any zero. A high total does not cure a wrong jurisdiction or date.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authority | Informal or unknown | Persuasive/administrative | Controlling primary authority |
| Authenticity | Provenance unknown | Reputable republication | Official/authorised publisher |
| Currency | Unchecked | Date shown, later change not checked | Version, commencement, amendments/treatment checked |
| Completeness | Snippet/summary | Partial document | Full relevant document and context |
| Relevance | General background | Related proposition | Direct support for issue and facts |
| Citability | No stable identifier | Identifiable but weak pinpointing | Stable citation, provision/paragraph, and link |
| Access | Unavailable/unverifiable | Restricted or unstable | Open and reproducibly accessible |

## Decision labels

- **Preferred:** primary/official, current, complete, directly relevant, and traceable.
- **Accept with qualification:** useful but unofficial, persuasive, restricted, incomplete, or subject to a documented currency limit.
- **Discovery only:** use to locate stronger sources; do not base a material proposition on it when a better source is available.
- **Reject:** unverifiable, contradicted, fabricated, obsolete for the relevant date, or outside the governing jurisdiction.

Record publisher, jurisdiction, subject coverage, source type, authority level, official status, typical use, limitations, preferred citation, currency method, and verification date.

## Negative propositions

A negative claim requires more than the absence of a search hit. Record the repositories, date range, jurisdictions, identifiers, search terms, and source limitations checked. Use a categorical claim only when an authoritative and sufficiently complete source establishes it. Otherwise report the bounded result, such as `no matching official authority was located`, and assign source-completeness confidence separately from confidence in any substantive conclusion.
