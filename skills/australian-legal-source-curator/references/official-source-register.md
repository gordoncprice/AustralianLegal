# Official Australian legal source register

Verified 18 July 2026. This is a starting register, not a substitute for task-specific verification. Open the source, confirm its current publisher and coverage, and record a fresh check. Prefer an authorised PDF where the site distinguishes it from HTML or Word versions.

## Commonwealth

| Publisher/source | Coverage and type | Authority/status and typical use | Currency check and limitations | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Federal Register of Legislation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/) | Acts, legislative/notifiable instruments, compilations, lifecycle data, gazettes and associated material | Authorised Commonwealth register; controlling starting point for legislation | Check in-force status, latest and point-in-time compilations, endnotes, amendment/commencement tables, pending amendments, disallowance and sunsetting. “In force” can include a made instrument awaiting commencement | Act/instrument title, year, `(Cth)`, provision, compilation or as-made identifier where material |
| [High Court judgments](https://www.hcourt.gov.au/cases-and-judgments/judgments) | High Court reasons and case material | Official Court publication; controlling apex authority | Check the case page, later orders/corrections and subsequent treatment. Pre-1998 unreported web coverage is incomplete; summaries are not reasons | Case name, authorised report if verified, neutral citation and paragraph |
| [Federal Court Digital Law Library](https://www.fedcourt.gov.au/digital-law-library/judgments) | Federal Court and Full Court judgments; related administered collections | Official Court source, updated regularly | Check latest/corrected judgment and [Federal Law Search](https://www.fedcourt.gov.au/online-services/federal-law-search) for proceeding status. Publication can be restricted | Case, neutral citation, court, paragraph |
| [Federal Circuit and Family Court judgments](https://www.fcfcoa.gov.au/judgments) | Divisions 1 and 2 family and general federal law judgments | Official Court source | Check current judgment and proceeding record. Not all filed or interlocutory material is public | Case, neutral citation, division/court, paragraph |
| [Administrative Review Tribunal](https://www.art.gov.au/) and [published decisions](https://www.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdb/au/cases/cth/ART/) | Commonwealth merits review and Guidance and Appeals Panel material | ART is the current tribunal; its publication policy uses AustLII for published decisions | Only selected decisions are published; check later Federal Court appeals. Use the former AAT only for historical matters | Matter name, neutral citation/identifier and paragraph |
| [ASIC regulatory resources](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/regulatory-resources-search/) and [registers](https://www.asic.gov.au/online-services/search-asic-registers/) | Corporations, financial services, insolvency, markets, regulatory guides, instruments and registers | Official corporate regulator. Guidance explains approach but is not legislation | Check update, registration and withdrawal status; verify legislative instruments on the Federal Register; some extracts are paid or limited | ASIC, document title/number/date or register extract date; legislation separately |
| [Fair Work Commission](https://www.fwc.gov.au/) [document search](https://www.fwc.gov.au/document-search) | Awards, agreements, decisions, orders and annual wage review | Official national workplace-relations tribunal | Check current instrument version, variation history, operative dates, agreement status, appeal and Full Bench treatment. Nominal expiry does not itself end an agreement | Instrument title/ID/clause or case neutral citation/paragraph |
| [Fair Work Ombudsman](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/) | Compliance guidance, award/pay resources and calculators | Official regulator guidance, not the controlling Act, award or agreement | Record effective/publication date and relevant pay period; verify instrument with FWC. Guidance may lag and classification is fact-sensitive | FWO, page/document, date and URL; cite controlling law separately |
| [Attorney-General’s Department — Legal system](https://www.ag.gov.au/legal-system) | Administrative law, federal courts, legal services policy and publications | Official department; legal effect depends on document type | Check issue/revision and supersession; verify operative directions or instruments on the Federal Register | Department, title, version/date and URL |
| [Safe Work Australia](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/law-and-regulation/model-whs-laws) | Model WHS laws and national policy | Official policy body; model text is not directly operative | Check the separately enacted jurisdictional law and local regulator. Note Victoria's distinct OHS regime | SWA, model material/date; cite local enactment for law |
| [ATO Legal Database](https://www.ato.gov.au/law) | Rulings, determinations, compliance guidelines, practice statements and impact statements | Official tax administrator; binding effect varies by document type | Check current/withdrawn status, date of effect, addenda, errata and replacement. Courts and legislation control | ATO document type/number/title/date and pinpoint |
| [Australian Human Rights Commission](https://humanrights.gov.au/about-us/what-we-do/legal/commission-guidelines) | Discrimination/human-rights guidance, exemptions, reports and complaint material | Official statutory commission; most guidance and conciliation summaries are not precedent | Check publication/expiry, later decisions and Gazette notices; many outcomes are confidential | AHRC, title/type/date and URL; cite statute/court separately |
| [Australian Parliament Bills and Legislation](https://www.aph.gov.au/bills), [ParlInfo](https://parlinfo.aph.gov.au/), and [Bills Digests](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Bills_Legislation/Bills_Digests) | Bills, explanatory memoranda, speeches, Hansard, committee reports and analysis | Official parliamentary record; extrinsic/secondary weight varies | Confirm bill version, amendments, passage, assent, all supplementary/replacement memoranda and final Act. A Bill is not law | Document/chamber/date/page or paragraph and URL |
| [Commonwealth Gazettes](https://www.legislation.gov.au/Gazettes) | Proclamations, notices, appointments and commencement evidence | Official Register publication | Reconcile commencement clause, registration, Gazette notice and compilation endnotes; contingent/staggered commencement needs further analysis | Gazette series/notice/date/identifier |
| [AustLII Commonwealth](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/cth/) and [LawCite](https://www.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/LawCite) | Broad republication, historical collections and citation discovery | Independent and unofficial, except that ART designates it to publish selected decisions | Check database update note and verify legislation with the Federal Register and decisions with issuer. Accuracy, completeness and currency are not guaranteed | Cite the underlying law; include AustLII URL only as supplementary access |

Corporate law is primarily Commonwealth law through the corporations legislation and ASIC, but state and territory courts, limitation/procedure, property, contract, equity, associations and applied-law questions can still matter.

For every state and territory row below, the section heading supplies jurisdiction; the linked name supplies publisher/organisation and official URL; the category supplies subject coverage and source type; and the final column records official status, typical use, currency method and limitations. Prefer these citation methods: legislation by short title, year, jurisdiction and provision; cases by name, verified authorised/neutral citation and paragraph; tribunal material by matter, identifier and pinpoint; guidance by issuing body, title, version/date and URL; and gazettes by series, issue/notice, date and page or identifier. Cite the underlying authority rather than AustLII when an official version is available.

## Cross-jurisdiction rules

- Use [AustLII's database index](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/databases.html) and LawCite for broad discovery, historical material and citation tracing, then verify with the issuing body. AustLII is generally not official or authorised and may lag or omit material.
- Overlay Fair Work Commission, Fair Work Ombudsman and Safe Work Australia sources only after testing which employment or safety system applies.
- For local corporate-law questions, research the Commonwealth corporations regime first but retain state/territory courts, procedure, limitation, property, contract, equity, associations, licensing and enforcement connections.
- Gazette evidence must be reconciled with the legislation register, commencement clause, current/historical compilation and transitional provisions.
