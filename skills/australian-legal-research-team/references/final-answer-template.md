# Final answer template

Use only the sections needed. Retain jurisdiction, authority, uncertainty, date, and disclaimer only to the extent material to the question. Internal workflow artefacts and review findings are not separate final-answer sections unless requested.

1. **Short answer** — direct and calibrated. Put an outcome-determinative assumption or unresolved coverage issue before the conclusion it qualifies.
2. **Governing law** — applicable and overlapping regimes, current legislation, cited basis, assumptions and material statutory operation.
3. **Principal authorities** — for every significant authority give the hyperlinked case name and neutral citation, court, precedential status, proposition supported, analytical purpose and verified pinpoint where relevant. Omit cumulative cases.
4. **Application** — apply the cited rules to supplied facts or labelled inferences. Place supporting authority immediately after every material application statement.
5. **Qualifications and exceptions** — contrary or limiting authority, exclusions, burdens where material, fact sensitivity, uncertainty and transitions, each cited at the point of support.
6. **Conclusion** — answer the question with immediate support from the controlling principles and strongest useful application authority.
7. **Research completeness** — where appropriate, identify the authority categories considered and any bounded gap, for example `Authorities considered included ...` or `No materially relevant later appellate authority was located in [repositories searched] as at [date]`. Do not imply exhaustive absence unless established.
8. **Recent developments** — material amendments, commencement/transitional issues, appellate developments, or regulator policy changes.
9. **Practical next steps** — immediate factual enquiries, documents required, information still needed, critical deadlines and potential procedural risks, without definitive tailored advice.
10. **Uncertainties and additional facts** — only outcome-determinative and materially relevant gaps: issue, reason, potential legal impact, priority, information required, and authority where the uncertainty depends on law or sourced material.
11. **Additional issues if relevant** — peripheral regimes only when facts make them material.
12. **Sources** — traceable primary sources first, then labelled official guidance and secondary material; disclose material single-authority dependency.
13. **Research date** — date currency and links were checked.
14. **Legal information disclaimer** — concise and proportionate.

For a narrow question, combine items 1–7 into a brief answer plus material qualifications and sources. For a simple statutory question, answer in a few paragraphs: direct rule, controlling provision, material assumption or exception, research date and concise disclaimer. For a next-24-hours request, use `Essential now` and `Useful follow-up`; prioritise documents, dates, preservation and immediate instructions and avoid a general legal memorandum. For a formal memorandum, retain full headings and an executive summary.

The first six headings are the default for a substantive answer. Minor questions may combine or omit headings, but not their underlying content. Cite each material rule, sourced observation, authority characterisation, statutory description, application, qualification, exception, uncertainty and conclusion immediately after the sentence or clause supported. Do not leave citation deserts or use one paragraph-end citation to mask distinct claims. Hyperlink every cited statute and case directly to a verified source.
