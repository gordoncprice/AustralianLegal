# Final answer template

Use only the sections needed, but retain jurisdiction, authority, uncertainty, date, and disclaimer.

1. **Research question** — neutral formulation.
2. **Brief answer** — direct and calibrated.
3. **Applicable jurisdiction** — likely and overlapping regimes, assumptions, confidence.
4. **Relevant legal framework** — current primary rules and their status.
5. **Analysis** — proposition, authority, application, limitation, conclusion.
6. **Important exceptions or qualifications** — exclusions, contrary authority, fact sensitivity, transitions.
7. **Practical implications** — research-based next considerations, not definitive tailored advice.
8. **Unresolved facts or uncertainty** — what could change the answer and next checks.
9. **Sources** — traceable primary sources first, then labelled official guidance and secondary material.
10. **Research date** — date currency and links were checked.
11. **Legal information disclaimer** — concise and proportionate.

For a narrow question, combine items 1–8 into a brief answer plus qualifications and sources. For a formal memorandum, retain full headings and an executive summary.
