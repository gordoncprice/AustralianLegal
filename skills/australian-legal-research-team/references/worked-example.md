# Worked example: remote employee redundancy

## Intake

Question: what obligations may apply to a redundancy affecting an employee who works remotely from New South Wales for a Victorian employer? Unknowns include the employing entity, constitutional-corporation status, award/agreement coverage, work history, job location agreement, group redeployment options, employee count, consultation steps, and whether the role is genuinely no longer required.

## Jurisdiction role

Map the Fair Work national system first without assuming coverage. Separately test New South Wales long service leave, workers' compensation, safety and discrimination connections; Victorian connections; contract terms; and any public-sector or residual-system exception. Do not choose law solely from the employer's address or the employee's home.

## Source role

Plan for the current *Fair Work Act 2009* (Cth), operative award/agreement and variations, Fair Work Commission decisions and orders, Fair Work Ombudsman guidance labelled as guidance, and official New South Wales and Victorian legislation sources for preserved local subjects. Include commencement and case-treatment checks.

## Research role

Research national-system coverage, National Employment Standards redundancy and consultation, genuine redundancy, unfair dismissal exclusions, award/agreement consultation and redeployment, plus each verified local overlay. List contrary or limiting cases and fact dependencies. Produce a cited draft without final approval.

## Review role

Challenge the employing-entity assumption, national-system coverage, operative instrument, employee-count rules, event date, territorial reach, redeployment group, source currency, and any statement that guidance has legal force. Require revisions for every unsupported conclusion.

## Final synthesis

Answer conditionally, identify the likely national framework and possible local overlays, list the facts that could materially change coverage or entitlements, cite current official sources, state the research date, and recommend tailored advice where timing or dismissal risk is significant.
