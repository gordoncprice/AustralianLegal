# Orchestration workflow

## Stage gates

1. **Intake and issue completeness:** freeze the question, material facts, assumptions, event date, deliverable, and risk level. Ask what legal issues could reasonably arise; test the proportionate issue checklist and exclude irrelevant issues.
2. **Jurisdiction map:** identify likely and possible regimes, exclusions, missing facts, overlap warnings, and confidence.
3. **Source plan:** identify official primary and secondary sources, court/tribunal databases, authority weight, stronger-authority checks, change-detection sources, currency methods, and access limits.
4. **Research and change detection:** build proposition ledgers for legislation, cases, guidance, and contrary material. For every material issue check recent amendments, commencement/transitions, recent controlling and appellate authority, and policy change where relevant. Record source dependency and structured uncertainty. Parallel streams must use the same facts, date, and handoff schema.
5. **Jurisdiction audit:** test the completed draft, not just the intake.
6. **Independent review:** give the reviewer the frozen draft and evidence bundle. The reviewer must not have authored a research stream and must not be instructed to confirm the result.
7. **Revision:** maintain an issue log. Address every critical and major item, disclose unresolved medium-risk issues, and remove unsupported claims.
8. **Final proposition audit:** after revision, recheck every material proposition for strongest reasonably available authority, exact source support, overbreadth, missing qualifications, currency and recent change, legal-effect label, source dependency, and new unsupported content introduced during revision. Record completion.
9. **Quality gate:** confirm that the strongest reasonably available authority was used; no stronger relevant authority appears overlooked; every material proposition is supported; uncertainties are structured and prioritised; assumptions are separate from conclusions; guidance is distinguished from binding law; issue completeness and proposition audits are complete; and the reviewer self-audit is complete.
10. **Delivery:** use a proportionate final structure, traceable sources, research date, separate confidence dimensions, uncertainty register, practical next steps, and legal-information disclaimer.

## Parallel boundaries

Suitable parallel streams include legislation/commencement, cases/treatment, regulator guidance/enforcement, and distinct jurisdiction overlays. Do not parallelise tasks whose inputs depend on unresolved earlier findings. Reconcile duplicates and conflicts explicitly; do not vote between agents.

## Sequential fallback

When specialist agents are unavailable, complete and save each artefact before changing roles. At minimum preserve headings for jurisdiction assessment, source plan, researcher draft, reviewer findings, revision log, final proposition audit, quality gate, and final synthesis. The review pass must reopen material sources and challenge the frozen draft.

## Stop conditions

Do not deliver as verified research when a controlling source cannot be found, jurisdiction cannot be responsibly narrowed, a critical review issue remains, or current-law status is unknown. Provide the useful preliminary result, explain the limit, reduce confidence, and identify the exact next verification step.
