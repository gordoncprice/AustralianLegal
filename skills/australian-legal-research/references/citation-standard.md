# Citation standard

Use the Australian Guide to Legal Citation where practicable, while prioritising traceability over cosmetic perfection.

## Legislation

Give the short title and year, jurisdiction abbreviation, and pinpoint provision: `Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 389`. Identify the compilation or historical version in the source note when currency or timing is material.

## Cases and tribunals

Give case name, authorised report citation where verified, neutral citation, court or tribunal, year/date as appropriate, and paragraph pinpoint: `Example v Example [2026] HCA 1, [42]`. Do not invent an authorised report citation.

## Other materials

Identify the issuing body, title, document type/date, pinpoint if available, official URL, and access/verification date when content is mutable. Label explanatory memoranda, parliamentary debates, regulator guidance, and commentary by type.

## Minimum checks

- Open the source and confirm title, jurisdiction, provision or paragraph, and text.
- Attribute every direct quotation and reproduce it exactly and sparingly.
- Ensure the source supports the full proposition, not merely adjacent words.
- Distinguish primary, official secondary, and unofficial material.
- Prefer links to official pages; add a recognised repository only when useful.
- State that a primary source was not located rather than substituting an unattributed summary.
