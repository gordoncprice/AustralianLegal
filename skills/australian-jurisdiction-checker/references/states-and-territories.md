# States and territories

Use these prompts for issue spotting, then verify the current law from official sources.

| Jurisdiction | Employment and workplace prompts | Other prompts |
|---|---|---|
| Commonwealth | National-system employer/employee, Fair Work instruments, superannuation, federal discrimination, migration, Commonwealth public sector | Corporations, insolvency, financial services, competition, privacy, tax |
| New South Wales | State/public and local government coverage, long service leave, WHS, workers' compensation, anti-discrimination | State contracts, licensing, property, court/tribunal jurisdiction |
| Victoria | National system plus preserved local laws, long service leave, OHS rather than model WHS terminology, workers' compensation, equal opportunity | State statutory and common-law causes, VCAT jurisdiction |
| Queensland | State/public and local government coverage, long service leave interactions, WHS, workers' compensation, anti-discrimination | QCAT and state court jurisdiction; do not infer Queensland from user location |
| Western Australia | National-system status and non-national-system employers, state industrial relations, long service leave, WHS, workers' compensation | Local corporations/associations and SAT jurisdiction |
| South Australia | National system, long service leave, WHS, workers' compensation, equal opportunity, SAET jurisdiction | State licensing and court jurisdiction |
| Tasmania | National system, long service leave, WHS, workers' compensation, anti-discrimination, public sector | TASCAT and state court jurisdiction |
| Australian Capital Territory | Territory employment connections, public sector, long service leave, WHS, workers' compensation, discrimination | ACAT; Commonwealth employment may coexist geographically |
| Northern Territory | Territory employment, long service leave, WHS, workers' compensation, anti-discrimination, public sector | NTCAT and territory court jurisdiction |

## Cross-border facts

For remote, mobile, seconded, fly-in/fly-out, platform, and multi-site workers, record where work is actually performed, contractual location, employer entities, supervision, payroll, injury or adverse conduct, and any governing-law or industrial-instrument terms. A contract clause does not by itself settle statutory territorial operation.

For companies, distinguish incorporation/registration from principal place of business, where business is carried on, where conduct and loss occurred, and the forum or regulator involved.
